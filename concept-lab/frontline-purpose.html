Selection rule

Choose the icon that still communicates “trustworthy forecasting” after the wordmark, color accents, and app UI are removed.

  1. Use blue triangles and red semicircles only when they describe an actual weather front.
  2. Keep the primary silhouette simple enough for embroidery and a 16px favicon.
  3. Let the product interface carry complexity; let the mark carry confidence.
